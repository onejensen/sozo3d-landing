## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The page is a hostile-brand product sleeve for a printing service: density itself is the material. It refuses the two arrangements this project has already rejected — the category's dark hero with an orange gradient wash and a grid of icon cards (the incumbent), and the instrument panel that read as music gear rather than a workshop. Here nothing glows, nothing is an appliance, and the whole surface is flat printed information crowded to the edge, with one enormous condensed word shouting through it.

**OWN-WORLD:** Absolute black ground with the site's own `#171717` as the second black. Flat print surfaces only, zero gradients, zero glow. 1px hairline rules build a modular frame that touches the page edges; cells butt against their neighbours. Vertical rails run down both margins carrying marks: the Sozo3D roundel, hazard stripes, a barcode, a version string, vertical set type. Ornament is a system, not decoration — every section and service carries a real REF code, and the registration crosses, hazard blocks and barcodes are drawn SVG in one consistent weight. **Two declared translations from the source world:** its magenta `#FF00A6` and safety orange `#FF5A00` collapse into the pinned brand orange `#E87722`, which becomes the single accent (the user pinned the site's own colours, so no second hot colour enters); and its faux-kanji ornament is replaced by Spanish industrial marking — REF., LOTE, part codes — because a Spanish workshop wearing Japanese lettering is a costume, not a system. Type: Big Shoulders Display for the shouting word, Archivo for prose, Martian Mono for codes, labels and values. Body prose is sentence case even though the source sets everything in caps: clarity wins that conflict, and caps stay on labels.

**STORY:** The visitor understands in one line that this is custom 3D printing in Spain, sees the four things it prints and the three reasons to trust it, and writes to `info@sozo3d.es`. The density has to earn attention without ever hiding the offer or the action — a committed form that buries either has not finished translating.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A code strip runs the full width at the top — roundel, `SOZO3D//3D`, REF string, hazard block, and the email control outlined at the right. Below it, the incumbent headline set as the shouting word: `IMPRIME` filling the width in heavy condensed white, `TU IDEA EN 3D` beneath it, the second line carrying the orange. The existing sub sits under a hairline rule with a `REF` chip. The primary action is a filled orange control with corner brackets reading "Pedir presupuesto"; a secondary outlined control carries the address itself. Left and right vertical rails frame it all with marks. Nothing occupies the centre but the word.

**FORM:** The catalog challenger `tdr-info-noise-sleeve`, dealt on re-roll round 1 of seed `e9ccbbe6` and chosen by the user from a *competitive* verdict — I judged it to hold audience identification and lose product clarity. That losing axis is the build's job: the offer and the action must read in seconds despite the density, and the honest risk I published (that it returns to a black ground, which is where we came from) is answered by making the black flat and printed rather than atmospheric. Signature interaction: **declared density tiers.** Ornament layers drop in named steps as the viewport narrows — side rails, then the registration field, then the hazard strips — while the shouting word scales and never drops below its floor. On load the marks stamp in first and the word lands last, once, orchestrated; `prefers-reduced-motion` renders the stamped state with no animation. Raises carried in from declined challengers: a shared measured grid so nothing appears without scale (from the signal bench), the confirmed-versus-pending distinction set typographically (from the provenance ribbon), and the page centre left unmarked so the subject owns its own space (from the accretion disk).

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
